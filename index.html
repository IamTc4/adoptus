About AdoptUs Mumbai

Building a compassionate city where humans and animals coexist in harmony.

Who We Are

AdoptUs is a volunteer-run platform dedicated to the welfare of Mumbai's stray animals. We believe that technology can bridge the gap between a stray animal in need and a human willing to help.

Founded in 2024, we started with a simple idea: Visibility saves lives. By mapping strays in areas like Bandra, Andheri, and Colaba, we ensure no animal goes unnoticed or unfed.

Today, we are a growing community of feeders, rescuers, vets, and adopters working together to create a #ZeroStrayMumbai.

How We Help

Identify

Our GPS-enabled reporting tool allows anyone to document a stray's location and condition in seconds.

Coordinate

Local guardians receive alerts for nearby animals, ensuring food and medical aid reaches them fast.

Rehome

We verify potential adopters and connect them directly with rescuers, cutting out the middleman.
