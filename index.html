About AdoptUs

AdoptUs is a progressive web application built with a singular purpose: to leverage technology for animal welfare. We believe that technology should be accessible to everyone, which is why we've built this platform to be lightweight, fast, and free to use.

How It Works

  • Spot: See a stray animal? Snap a photo and upload it. Our compression tech ensures it works even on slow networks.
  • Locate: The GPS pin helps other guardians find the animal to provide food or care.
  • Adopt: Families looking for a pet can browse local rescues and connect directly via WhatsApp.
